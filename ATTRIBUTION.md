## Placement

- **Interactive covered applications** (the browser demo): the supplied
  persistent plain-text **Powered by Vulcan** link, or an equivalent prominent
  **Vulcan Community**, **About**, **Legal** or **Source** item, containing this
  notice, the full applicable terms and the exact Corresponding Source link.
- **Source distributions**: `README.md`, `NOTICE`, package metadata and the
  relevant source headers.
- **Headless tools** (the CLI): `NOTICE`, documentation and package metadata,
  plus `h3 --version` output.

Modified versions replace the source placeholder with their own exact source URL,
identify their modifications and preserve the origin and non-affiliation fields.
The notice and its navigation label may be translated, restyled or reformatted
without changing their substance or convenient prominence. No Vulcan logo is
required.
